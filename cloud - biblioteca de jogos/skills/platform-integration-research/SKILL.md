# Platform Integration Research

## Use when

- A platform may need official docs, SDKs or launcher-based integration.
- Compliance or privacy is uncertain.
- The project needs a factual research brief before implementation.

## Checklist

- Confirm official sources.
- Separate official, unofficial and local approaches.
- Capture auth, data availability and restrictions.
- Record risks and the next recommendation.
